2 What data we collect and why

2.1 Delivery addresses

To fulfil your print order we collect the recipient name and postal address you enter in the app. This is passed to our printing partner (Prodigi) who prints and ships your photos. Without this data we cannot complete your order.

  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention: 7 years to satisfy accounting and tax obligations, then deleted

2.2 Photos

Photos you select for printing are temporarily uploaded to our servers (Supabase, hosted in the EU) and forwarded to Prodigi for production. We do not analyse, share, or use your photos for any purpose other than fulfilling your order.

  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention: Deleted automatically within 30 days after your order is placed

2.3 Payment and subscription data

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, Stripe collects your card details, billing address, and e-mail address. We never see or store your full card number. We receive and store a Stripe customer ID and your e-mail address (returned by Stripe after checkout) to identify your subscription and respond to support requests.

  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention: Stripe retains billing records as required by financial law. We delete our copy of your e-mail and customer ID upon a verified deletion request.

2.4 Feedback messages

If you submit feedback through the app we collect your message text, app and device version, and — if available — your e-mail address so we can reply. Feedback is stored in our database and forwarded to our inbox via the Resend e-mail service.

  • Legal basis: Legitimate interest in improving our service and responding to users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • Retention: 2 years, then deleted

2.5 App diagnostics

We do not integrate any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Basic device and OS version strings are included with feedback submissions only when you actively send feedback. We do not track you across apps or websites.

6 Your rights under GDPR

As a person in the EU/EEA you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right of access (Art. 15) Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to rectification (Art. 16) Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to erasure (Art. 17) Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
Right to restriction (Art. 18) Ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
Right to data portability (Art. 20) Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to object (Art. 21) Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
✉️
To exercise any of these rights, e-mail memorymailer@agencyagency.at with the subject line "GDPR Request". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. As the controller is based in Austria, the lead authority is the Datenschutzbehörde (DSB), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna — www.dsb.gv.at. If you are located in another EU member state you may also contact the authority there.

7 Data security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Data at rest is stored in Supabase (EU region), which encrypts storage at the infrastructure level. Access to our database is restricted by role-based access controls and API key authentication. We review our security measures regularly.

8 Children's privacy

Memory Mailer is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
